# Civ 6 Mod List and Compatibility Check

Ten mods from two YouTube videos, with grammar cleaned up from the auto-transcripts, followed by a compatibility check between the mods and against the expansions.

---

## Part 1: The 10 mods

### Video 1: Five mods that change how Civ 6 plays

1. **Movable Districts** (by wltk)
   Spend gold to move a district you've already built to a new tile. While a city's production panel is open, each movable district shows a gear Move button on its tile. The cost is twice the district's original production cost, so a 50-production district costs 100 gold to move. The district isn't destroyed and rebuilt: it moves with all its buildings intact. Only main one-per-city districts are supported. Neighborhoods, Canals, and Dams can't be moved. This matters because district placement, one of the biggest tactical decisions in Civ 6, used to be permanent.

2. **Deity++** (difficulty mod) — **on hold. Owner swapped it for Real Strategy (AI) on 2026-11-09**, preferring smarter opponents over bonus-fed ones. It can be added later; the two don't conflict.
   Adds two difficulty tiers above Deity: Deity+ and Deity++. Vanilla Deity already gives the AI multipliers on production and gold, faster science, culture, and faith, extra early units and builders, and a combat-strength bonus. Deity++ extends that same progression further. It does **not** make the AI smarter. It only gives the AI more resources and bonuses, so opponents don't plan better, but they get far more early momentum.

3. **Resource pack** (four mods grouped as one entry)
   - **Sukritact's Oceans**
   - **Sukritact's Resources**
   - **Latin American Resources** (by Leugi)
   - **Reindeer**

   On paper these don't change the balance much, but they make every map feel fresher and make tile evaluation more interesting. Sukritact's Oceans is the standout for naval and archipelago games: it adds new sea resources (such as seals, squid, coral, kelp forests, and lake caviar) and makes water tiles worth working. Coastal cities get better yields and more to improve without being overpowered, and everything looks like it could have shipped in the base game. The capybara is a highlight: once improved, it gives very strong food and gold. The new luxuries also work with the Monopolies and Corporations game mode, so they can form industries and monopolies.

4. **Workable 4th Ring** (by PhantomJ_M) — **confirmed by owner.** Workshop ID: TBD.
   Removes the base game's three-tile working radius. In vanilla, your borders can grow to the fourth or fifth ring, but citizens can't work those tiles, so that land is economically useless. This mod lets cities work tiles in the fourth ring at full yields. It also opens up aggressive play: an Encampment four tiles from your city center can sit just two tiles from an enemy city, making a forward military base you can also work tiles around. Overall it raises how big cities can scale, makes late border growth worth more, and allows more creative district placement.

5. **Civilizations Expanded + Steel and Thunder: Unit Expansion** (two mods used together)
   Civilizations Expanded reworks civilizations, leaders, and abilities, often adding new mechanics or boosting the originals. Steel and Thunder adds many units across the eras, fills gaps between techs, and gives many civs extra unique units. Examples from the video:
   - **Russia** can buy districts with faith in cities that have a governor, which makes its already strong faith economy expand very fast.
   - **England** gets the Ship of the Line, which replaces the Frigate and gains up to +7 combat strength next to another Ship of the Line. That makes naval domination even stronger on water-heavy maps.
   - **China** gets an early support unit in the Classical era that speeds up experience gain and adds combat strength to nearby units.

   Together they shift power spikes and timing windows, so some civs feel almost unfair when played well and are frightening in the hands of an AI that snowballs.

### Video 2: Five quality-of-life mods

6. **Radial Measuring Tool** (by Zur13) — **installed.** Workshop ID 1628605090.
   Measures exact tile distances on the map. Click the RMT button in the top-right corner, pick a starting tile, and drag to see the range. This is useful for wonders with an area effect (Temple of Artemis, the Colosseum), district placement, and city spacing, with no hex counting by hand.

7. **City-state quest screen** (likely **Better City States (UI)** by infixo; the video didn't name it)
   Puts every city-state quest on one screen, so you can see at a glance what each one wants: a trade route, a building, a boost, or a converted city. Because it's all in one place, you naturally finish more quests and earn more envoys. It doesn't change any mechanics; it just removes friction.

8. **Enhanced Camera** (by DahakaMVl) — **installed.** Workshop ID 2484412297.
   Unlocks the camera so you can zoom out much farther to see your whole empire or a continent, and zoom in closer than usual to look at wonders up close. It pairs well with visual mods that make cities look denser as they grow.

9. **Policy change reminder** (exact mod not confirmed; a mod named **Policy Change Reminder** exists)
   Reminds you every time you can swap policies for free, so you don't run the wrong setup for twenty turns after unlocking a civic.

10. **Auto-repeat projects** — **Repeat Project** by yamada, **confirmed by owner** (ID 1505351262; UI mod, updated Jul 2021, 776 ratings). Alternative not used: Non Stop Project by Flavian.
    Lets a city project repeat on its own when it finishes, such as Campus Research Grants, Industrial Zone projects, and Theater Square festivals. This cuts late-game micromanagement and keeps your yields and great-person points steady.

**The big one: Civilizations Expanded needs a lot of DLC.** The author lists these as required:
- Rise and Fall
- Gathering Storm
- Aztec Civilization Pack
- Poland Civilization & Scenario Pack
- Australia Civilization & Scenario Pack
- Persia and Macedon Civilization & Scenario Pack
- Nubia Civilization & Scenario Pack
- Khmer and Indonesia Civilization & Scenario Pack

New Frontier Pass content is optional. If you own only one or two expansions, **Civilizations Expanded is the mod most likely to be off the table.** Steel and Thunder works on its own without it.

### What to check when you get home

Open Civ 6 → Additional Content → DLC, and note which of these you have:
- Rise and Fall
- Gathering Storm
- New Frontier Pass (adds game modes including Monopolies and Corporations, Secret Societies, Heroes and Legends, Apocalypse, Dramatic Ages, Barbarian Clans, Zombie Defense, and Tech and Civic Shuffle)
- Leader Pass
- The six civ packs listed above

Monopolies and Corporations matters for the resource mods: their new luxuries can form industries only if you have that game mode.

### Mod-to-mod compatibility

| Pair or group | Status | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| Civilizations Expanded + Steel and Thunder | **Confirmed compatible** | Civilizations Expanded blocks its own conflicting unit changes when Steel and Thunder is on. Unit text may still describe the Civilizations Expanded version. |
| Steel and Thunder + Moar Units | **Confirmed conflict** | Not on your list, but don't add it. Steel and Thunder replaces it. |
| Civilizations Expanded + other civ reworks | **Confirmed conflict** | Also conflicts with any mod that lets all civs work mountains or changes the Ski Resort. None of your 10 do this. |
| Deity++ + everything else | **Likely compatible** | Author: it replaces no files and changes only difficulty settings. Only conflicts with other difficulty mods. |
| The four resource mods together | **Likely compatible** | Sukritact and Leugi work together often, and these are commonly run as a set. More resource types means each one spawns less often. |
| Sukritact's Oceans + Yet (Not) Another Maps Pack | **Reported risk** | Not on your list. Matters only if you add map packs. |
| Resource mods + Civ6 Plus or CIVITAS Resources | **Reported** | Missing resource icons. Not on your list. |
| Better City States (UI) + CIVITAS City-States | **Confirmed conflict** | Not on your list. Both replace the City States screen. |
| Better City States (UI) + CQUI | **Confirmed compatible** | Relevant if you also run CQUI. |
| **Movable Districts + Repeat Project** | **Tested OK** (2026-11-09, with CQUI) | Predicted risk did not happen: both add their own UI contexts instead of replacing the production panel file. See `docs/playtests/002-group2.md`. |
| **Workable 4th Ring + other city UI mods** | **Risk** | A similar mod (CypRyan's Workable Outer Rings) had reported conflicts with mods that change citizen assignment, and its author said districts in the outer rings weren't supported. Expect the same kind of edge cases. |
| Workable 4th Ring + Movable Districts | **Unknown** | Moving a district into the 4th ring may or may not be allowed. Test it. |
| Repeat Project + Non Stop Project | **Don't run both** | They do the same job. |
| Enhanced Camera + other camera mods | **Likely conflict** | Camera mods edit the same camera file. Run only one. |
| Movable Districts in multiplayer | **Reported** | Players reported desyncs and lost Great Works after a move. Single-player is safer. |

### Install order that keeps testing simple

1. **Quality-of-life first:** Radial Measuring Tool, Better City States, camera, policy reminder. Start a game and check that each button or screen appears.
2. **Add Repeat Project, then Movable Districts.** Check that both the repeat option and the Move button show in the production panel.
3. **Add Workable 4th Ring.** Grow a city to the 4th ring and assign a citizen there.
4. **Add the four resource mods.** Start a new map and look for new resources.
5. **Add Real Strategy (AI)** (replaces Deity++ per owner, 2026-11-09). Test it in the same group as Workable 4th Ring. Check for no Lua errors, and that the AI settles and builds normally.
6. **Add Steel and Thunder, then Civilizations Expanded** if you own the DLC.

If a game bounces you back to the menu or crashes on load, open `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Firaxis Games\Sid Meier's Civilization VI\Logs\` (older guides say Documents; that changed in the Leader Pass update) and read `Database.log` and `Modding.log`. The last mod you added is the likely cause.

### Caveats

- Several mods weren't named in the video (items 7 through 10, plus Reindeer and Workable 4th Ring). The names above are my best match. Check each video's description for the exact links.
- When I opened Steam pages, every mod page showed a "removed" banner, including big mods like Civilizations Expanded. That points to my tool, not the mods. Confirm each page loads normally for you.
- Most of these mods haven't been updated since 2020 to 2023. Civ 6's final patch came out around then, so old mods usually still work.

## Part 3: Other investigations

Mods outside the original 10. Each one notes how it relates to the goal: a tuned, compatible Civ 6 setup now, and the rule ledger for your own tile-based game later.

### Combo packs (mods that already bundle features)

1. **CQUI: Community Quick User Interface** (MIT license). Rating 9/10.
   - **What it bundles:** city-state quests shown inside the city-state panel, a popup when new policies are available, Better Trade Screen, More Lenses, Better Espionage Screen, Improved Deal Screen, Divine Yuri's Custom City Panel, and Simplified Gossip.
   - **How it relates:** it covers most of mod #7 (city-state quests) and #9 (policy reminder), so you may not need those two at all. Better City States (UI) is confirmed compatible with it. Because it's MIT-licensed, it's the one mod you can legally fold into a merged mod, with attribution. It's also a working example of what you'd be doing: absorbing other UI mods into one by hand-merging the files they replace.
   - **Watch for:** it changes the city panel, so test it alongside Movable Districts, Repeat Project, and Workable 4th Ring.

2. **Concise UI** (by eudaimonia). Rating 6/10.
   - **What it bundles:** a UI overhaul with city-state quests and envoy totals in the city-state panel, map tack upgrades, and an options menu that includes reminders. One commenter says it can repeat projects, but its own page doesn't say so.
   - **How it relates:** an alternative to CQUI that might also cover mod #10. It's a full UI overhaul, so **run Concise UI or CQUI, not both.**

3. **Civ6 Plus: Harmony in Diversity**. Rating 3/10 for this setup.
   - **What it bundles:** a large overhaul of civ abilities, terrain yields, map generation, and regional building effects.
   - **How it relates:** it competes with your list instead of adding to it. Its author says it is **incompatible with Civilizations Expanded** and doesn't work with CIVITAS City-States. Missing resource icons were also reported with Sukritact's mods. Worth studying as a design reference, not worth running.

4. **Better Balanced Game (BBG)**. Rating 5/10 for this setup.
   - **What it bundles:** a community rebalance of civs, leaders, and systems, still maintained and widely used in multiplayer.
   - **How it relates:** it likely clashes with Civilizations Expanded, since both rework civ abilities (my judgment, not confirmed). **Pick one.** BBG for tight balance, Civilizations Expanded for bigger and wilder civs. BBG's changes are also a useful study of what experienced players think the base game got wrong. That's good input for the rule ledger.

### Suggested shortlist

If you add only a few, add these in this order:
1. **CQUI**: replaces two of your 10 and is the safest base.
2. **Real Strategy (AI)**: fixes the "more stuff, not smarter" problem with Deity++.
3. **Detailed Map Tacks**: makes Movable Districts and the 4th ring worth planning around.
4. **Enhanced Mod Manager**: makes all the testing faster.

### How this ties to merging into one mod

- A full merge of all 10 is 5/10. A small patch mod that loads last and fixes only the conflicts is 8/10, and keeps each mod's updates.
- Starting from CQUI cuts the number of UI mods you'd have to hand-merge.
- Conflicts to resolve in a patch: Movable Districts with Repeat Project (production panel), and Workable 4th Ring with any city panel mod, CQUI included.
